Origin & Ethos

Not an online store.
A tactile catalog.

VOID is a small, independent clothing project focused on embroidered T-shirts and hoodies. We make our designs in small batches and focus on keeping the quality good while keeping our prices reasonable.

We make T-shirts and hoodies with designs embroidered directly onto the fabric. We keep the designs simple, the quality good, and the prices affordable.

How We Operate

This website serves exclusively as a showcase and product catalog for local selling. There is no automated shopping cart or third-party checkout gateway. Customers browse the available collection here and get in touch with us directly via WhatsApp or email to reserve or purchase items.
